About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Informatics (Bioinformatics track) at Indiana University Bloomington, advised by Haixu Tang, and I am also completing an M.S. in Computer Science. My research builds machine learning methods for longitudinal clinical data, multi-omics analysis, and privacy-preserving biomedical AI.

My dissertation develops a Virtual Patient framework: a hierarchical variational autoencoder with physiological-subsystem encoders and a Cox/DeepSurv survival head, trained on longitudinal MIMIC-IV and eICU intensive-care data with IU School of Medicine collaborators. I am extending it toward causal inference, risk assessment, and physiological aging. In parallel I lead cross-species metabolomics of aging, built MGPipe for shotgun metagenomics of gestational disease, and study mitochondrial genomic variation with All of Us data.

Before graduate school I spent three years in cGMP and CLIA/CAP/ISO molecular laboratories at Asuragen, Agilent Technologies, and Sonic Reference Laboratory, and I have completed two summers as an omics bioinformatics intern at Zoetis. I received my B.S. in Cell and Molecular Biology from The University of Texas at Austin, where I worked with Jeffrey Barrick.

On the job market. I finish my Ph.D. in May 2027 and am looking for industry roles in bioinformatics, computational biology, and biomedical ML starting June 2027.
